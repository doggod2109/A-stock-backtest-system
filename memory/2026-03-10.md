## User Education

**User Question:** "Difference between annual return and total return?"

**Explanation:**
- **Total Return:** Total percentage gain over entire period (e.g., 223% over 3 years)
- **Annual Return:** Average percentage gain per year, compound adjusted
- **Formula:** Annual = (1 + Total)^(1/years) - 1
- **Importance:** Annual return allows fair comparison across different time periods
